# Changelog

# Release rule

A package whose content changes gets a new version, even for a small fix. A tarball rebuilt under the same name and version is not picked up by a host that has already installed it: npm keeps its copy and the lockfile keeps the old hash, so a machine or a Docker build never sees the change. (The 0.3.1 `react` tarball was rebuilt after the Atelier had copied it, which is how this was found: `docs/proposals/atelier-reply-2026-11-16.md`.) If a rebuild under the same version cannot be avoided, the hand-off must say that the lockfile hash has to be updated and the package reinstalled.

## 0.4.8 (2026-11-27): plugin API: a location on warnings, several clicked points in a list, a title for the add group; Even facets in Low poly; settings dialogs keep Close in view

- **Plugin API: a warning can say where it happened.** A warning (`{ code, params }`) may carry `where: { points, triangles }`. The step's notes then show **Show on the model**, which lights the triangles and marks the points, and moves the view to "up to this step" when needed. The engine drops a `where` that is malformed or out of range (the warning stays), and caps it at 64 points and 20 000 triangles. Texts in en, nl and fr.
- **Plugin API: a list row may hold a placement**, so a step can take several clicked points (for example up to four keyholes). Each row has a button to click on the model; points follow earlier steps that move or scale the model, as a single placement does.
- **Add a step: the `add` group has a title** ("Add a part", with nl and fr), for plugins that add a part.
- **Low poly gets an "Even facets" style** (plugin `org.ateliermesh.lowpoly` 0.3.0), from micro-fabriek.be. The step has a new setting Style: Reduce (today's behaviour, the default; stored steps keep it) or Even facets. Even facets remeshes any closed model into even triangles of 4 to 40 mm and moves each corner in or out at random, so every facet catches the light; rims, bases and flat tops stay exactly flat, a least-wall check keeps the model printable, and the result stays a closed solid. Colours: one, alternating or random from 2 to 4, each a segment for multicolour printing. The same seed gives the same result bit for bit. Settings: facet size, jitter, sideways, seed, least wall, inside, plain bottom and top, colours. Texts in en, nl and fr; the method is in `docs/facets-method.md`.
- **A 3MF from Bambu Studio or Creality Print keeps the colour of each part.** The extruder of a part (`Metadata/model_settings.config`) is read, so a model made of coloured parts shows all its colours, not only the painted triangles (a four-colour Creality file showed two).

## Cheese 0.1.1
- plugins: `cheese` has calmer rims. The cut left slivers and folds around the openings: a surface vertex a hair from a sphere made a cut point 1e-6 from it (a zero-area triangle), and the rim rounding then turned such triangles over. Vertices within 10 % of the longest edge of a sphere are now moved onto it (radially; a vertex on the wall of an earlier hole slides along that wall to the circle where the two spheres meet, so creases stay exact), degenerate triangles are not made, a hole whose opening cannot be closed is cut again with a smaller move (and in the end none, as before) and the whole cut is repeated with smaller moves before a hole is dropped. ### orient
- plugins: new operation `orient` ("Orient for printing", category `prepare`) in the core plugin: the same search as the analyzer `auto_orient` (goal `min_support`, `min_height` or `max_stability`), but it turns the model the best way up and, with `place_on_bed` (default on), moves it so its lowest point is at Z = 0. The report names the angles, whether it turned, the lift, and the overhang and base areas. The analyzer `auto_orient` stays (a report only, category `check`).

### plugin `org.ateliermesh.core` 0.2.0 and the new plugins

- **Surface texture (new plugin `org.ateliermesh.texture` 0.1.0):** one operation, `texture`, moves the surface along its normal by a height pattern: 13 built-in tileable patterns (knurl, ridges, waves, dots, hexagonal dots, hexagons, bricks, weave, grid lines, noise, leather, wood grain, stipple) or a picture (PNG decoded by the plugin; JPEG through `createImageBitmap`), triplanar, planar, cylindrical, spherical and box mapping, a region (segment or group) with an edge fade, an optional segment for the textured triangles, and conforming per-edge subdivision to the pattern's feature size. A closed solid stays closed. New codes `texture.too_heavy`, `texture.may_self_intersect` (warning), `texture.no_image`, `texture.image_unsupported`, `texture.image_too_large`, `texture.bad_input`; order hints `texture_before_text` and `reduce_after_texture`. Clean implementation: no code or pictures of other tools.
- **Smooth surface (plugin `org.ateliermesh.deform` 0.2.0):** new operation `smooth`: Taubin lambda|mu smoothing of the vertices (no shrinking, no change to the triangles, groups or segments), with `strength`, `iterations`, `keep_edges` (creases stay crisp, smoothed along their length), `keep_volume`, `keep_borders`, `max_move` and an optional `part` with a `falloff`. Warning `smooth.thin_features`, stage `smooth`, order hints `smooth_after_text` and `smooth_before_surface`; the Voronoi pattern is now the only Voronoi step the order hints treat as a surface pattern (a Voronoi shell is a new shape). `twist` and `taper` are unchanged; stored steps of 0.1.0 still run.
- **Voronoi shell, wording (unreleased change):** the operation and its texts no longer say lamp or bulb; the sample is "Voronoi shell" (a tapered vase, open at the top, then smoothed) and `docs/samples/voronoi-lamp.3mf` is now `voronoi-shell-example.3mf`. The settings (`opening`, `opening_diameter` and the rest) are unchanged.
- **Voronoi shell (plugin `org.ateliermesh.voronoi` 0.2.0):** new operation `voronoi_shell` hollows a closed model into a Voronoi lattice (wall thickness, strut width, rounded joints, `cells` and `seed` personalisable, an optional round opening along Z with a collar round its edge). An implicit surface (signed distance, Voronoi field, marching tetrahedra, smoothing), so the result is always a closed manifold solid. Codes `voronoi_shell.*`, stages `pattern`, `field`, `surface`. The `voronoi` operation and stored steps are unchanged. `sphere(radius, subdivisions)` is exported next to `gridBox` and `frustum`.
- **Manifest presentation hints (core, react, plugins):** optional keys that let one general form draw every operation in the designed look. Per parameter: `widget` (`segmented`, `cards` for an enum; `slider`, `stepper` for a number; `axes` for a vector; `font`, `gallery` for an asset, as before), `importance` (`main`, `normal`, `advanced`), `icon`, and `help` is now checked; an enum option may be `{ value, label, icon }`. Per operation: `icon` and `summary` (a template such as `{{angle}}°`). `validateManifest` refuses a widget that does not fit the type (`bad_widget`), a slider without `min` and `max` (`slider_needs_range`), a bad `importance`, `icon` or `summary` (`bad_importance`, `bad_icon`, `bad_summary`, `summary_unknown_param`). The JSON Schema export carries them as `x-widget`, `x-importance` and `x-icon`. `manifest_version` stays 1: all keys are optional and a host that does not know a hint shows the plain control. `StepForm` in `@ateliermesh/react` is now the renderer for every operation (Size, Rotate, Twist, Taper, Repair, Low poly and a third-party plugin included); the generated panel remains for non-React hosts and for an operation with a painted `selection`. The built-in manifests carry the hints (UK English labels as option labels).
- **Step form groups (react):** the first group is open and the others are collapsed unless a section says `collapsed: false`; fields without a section form a "Settings" group at the top. `Field` draws vectors (X, Y, Z boxes), angles, sliders, plus and minus buttons, cards, and a list for an enum with more than four choices; `stepSummary` reads the manifest's `summary` first.
- **Preview pictures (core, standalone):** a project (`.amproj`) can carry `preview.png` (`ProjectState.preview`, returned by `loadProject`): a PNG of at most 1 MB and 1024 x 1024 px, checked by `previewProblem`; a writer refuses a bad one (`export.invalid_preview`), a reader leaves a bad one out. Older files still open. The standalone writes a 480 x 320 render of the current result into every project it saves (file, browser, autosave) and shows it on the start page and in the saved list. The sample cards use pre-rendered PNGs (`npm run samples:thumbs`) and a live render is remembered per sample definition.
- **Plugin crashes (core):** an unexpected exception in a plugin, a failed load, a dead worker or an unreadable result is returned as `plugin.crashed` (before: `plugin.failed` with `reason: 'worker_error'` or a message) and the runner switches the plugin off; its steps then fail with `plugin.disabled`. New: `Registry.setEnabled / isEnabled / disabledPlugins / subscribe / version`, `RunOptions.deactivateOnCrash / onPluginCrash`, `WorkflowResult.crashedPlugins`, `crashError`, `isUnexpected`, loader `probePackage`, React `CrashBanner`, `useRegistryVersion`, `PluginRow.disabled`, `PluginManagerProps.onToggle`. **Behaviour change:** a sandboxed plugin that throws now gives `plugin.crashed`, not `plugin.failed`.
- `emboss` is split in two operations on one engine: `emboss` (text) and `emboss_svg`. A stored step with `op: 'emboss'` and `source: 'svg'` migrates to `emboss_svg` (new `migrateOp` hook of a plugin module); the old `source` value is accepted and ignored on the text operation.
- New `mode: 'flat'`: the outline only gives the surface its segment; nothing is lifted, no walls, still a closed solid.
- New `svg_mode` (`fill` default, `line`). **Behaviour change:** before, fills and strokes were merged; now `fill` embosses the filled areas only and ignores strokes. A drawing with nothing to use in the chosen mode fails with `emboss.svg_empty_mode`.
- New `walls_segment` (default `text_walls`): the side walls are their own segment, separate from `segment` (top, floor or flat area). An empty `walls_segment` keeps one segment, as before. Projects saved before now get a `text_walls` segment in the table.

### plugin `org.ateliermesh.lowpoly` 0.1.0 (new, 2026-11-09)
- One operation, `low_poly` ("Low poly", `personalisable: 'never'`): an artistic version of the model made of few, large, flat facets.
